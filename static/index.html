    What this is

    A model that guesses what you are typing into batwan.org's search box: the rest of the word, or, after a space, the next one. It is a LoRA fine-tune of Gemma 4 E2B, taught on queries cut from Batwan's Hiligaynon dataset: its dictionary's words, their English, and phrases from its prose. It runs here on two CPU cores, so a suggestion takes a second or two.

    It suggests what is likely to be typed, not what is true or correct Hiligaynon. No speaker has reviewed it. This is a demo, not for commercial use.

    Using it from another page

    GET /api/suggest?q=maayong%20a&lang=any&limit=3

{"query": "maayong a", "lang": "any",
 "items": [{"type": "predict", "text": "maayong aga", "completion": "ga",
            "score": 0.62, "complete": false}, …],
 "ms": 840}

    lang is any, hil or en; limit up to 8; words (0–3) lets a suggestion run on past the word being typed. The shape is batwan.org's own /api/suggest's, with "type": "predict". Browsers may call it from batwan.org. Nothing typed is logged.
